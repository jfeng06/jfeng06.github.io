01 / ANALOG CIRCUITS

Traffic Signaling System

Sequencing green, yellow, and red with two 555 timers—and checking the timing at the bench.

Built & testedRead project report ↗Download PDF ↓

The challenge

Build a repeating traffic-light sequence from discrete timing circuits. The project connected monostable and astable timer theory to the practical work of selecting components, wiring a breadboard, and interpreting measured waveforms.

What I built

I used two LM555 timer ICs to sequence the green, yellow, and red LEDs. I calculated RC time constants, adjusted resistor and capacitor values, and compared oscilloscope traces with the expected timing behavior. Separate monostable and astable experiments established the timer behavior before the full circuit.

Bench validation

The monostable experiment measured a 3.21-second pulse against a 2.97-second calculation using the selected components. An astable experiment measured a 2.010-second period. For the traffic-light circuit, I examined individual phase traces and the repeating output sequence.

What I learned

Component tolerance and capacitor selection affect the behavior of a physical timing circuit. Troubleshooting strobing and overlapping states required interpreting both outputs together, rather than relying only on the ideal schematic.
